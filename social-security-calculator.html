When should you claim Social Security?

You can start as early as age 62, but your benefit is permanently reduced by about 30%. Wait until age 70, and your check grows by about 8% per year of delay.

If you expect to live into your 80s or 90s, delaying to 70 usually pays off. If you have health concerns or need the money now, claiming at 62 makes sense.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Claiming at 62 'because I can'

Your benefit is permanently reduced by about 30% if you claim at 62. If you live past your mid-80s, waiting until 70 pays off significantly.

Forgetting spousal benefits

A non-working spouse can claim up to 50% of the working spouse's benefit. If you're married, coordinate your claiming strategy.
