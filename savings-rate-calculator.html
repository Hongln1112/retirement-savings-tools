What's a good savings rate?

The US average savings rate is only around 3–5%, which means most people are living paycheck to paycheck.

Financial experts generally recommend: 10–15% for retirement, 20%+ for healthy financial habits, and 50%+ if you want to retire early (the FIRE movement).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Saving what's left, not spending what's left

The order matters: pay yourself first. Automate your savings to come out the same day you get paid. What's left is what you spend.

Ignoring small expenses

That $5 daily coffee adds up to $1,825 per year. Invested at 7% for 30 years, that's over $200,000. Small leaks sink big ships.
