Traditional vs. Roth IRA

Traditional IRA: You get a tax deduction now, pay taxes when you withdraw in retirement.

Roth IRA: You pay taxes now, withdraw tax-free in retirement. Better if you're early in your career and expect higher taxes later.

Not sure which is right? See our Roth vs. Traditional comparison.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Forgetting the contribution deadline

You can contribute to the prior year's IRA until April 15 of the next year. Many people miss this — it's a last-minute tax deduction.

Roth IRA income limits

If your income exceeds the phase-out range, you can't contribute directly. But a Backdoor Roth IRA conversion is still available regardless of income.
