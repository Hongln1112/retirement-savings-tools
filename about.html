About Retirement Savings Tools

We build free, browser-based calculators and clear guides to help you understand your money — without needing a financial advisor.

What we do

Retirement Savings Tools is a collection of free financial calculators plus practical guides. Our goal is simple: help you see the real numbers behind saving, investing, and retirement planning.

  • Calculate your 401(k), IRA, and retirement savings growth over time.
  • Figure out how much emergency fund you actually need.
  • See how compound interest works for — or against — you.
  • Understand social security, inflation, and net worth.

How it works

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter never leave your device — we don't store them, transmit them, or ask you to create an account. Just enter your numbers, see the result, and go.

Why we built this

Financial planning can feel overwhelming — jargon, spreadsheets, and conflicting advice everywhere. We believe the basics should be simple and free. Start with the numbers, understand what they mean, and then decide if you need professional help.

Important disclaimer

All calculators on this site provide estimates for general guidance only and are not financial, tax, or investment advice. Investment returns, contribution limits, and tax rules change over time. Always confirm details with a qualified financial professional before making decisions.
