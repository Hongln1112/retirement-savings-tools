Why the employer match matters

If your employer offers a 50% match on the first 6% of your salary, that's an instant 50% return on your money. Free money — never leave it on the table. At minimum, contribute enough to get the full match.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Leaving employer match on the table

If your employer matches 50% on the first 6% and you only contribute 3%, you're leaving thousands of dollars per year unclaimed. Always contribute at least enough to get the full match.

Looking at the balance, not the fees

A 0.50% expense ratio difference sounds tiny, but on a $500,000 portfolio over 30 years, it costs you well over $100,000. Check your fund fees.
