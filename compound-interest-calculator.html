Why compound interest is powerful

Einstein called it the "eighth wonder of the world." The longer you let money grow, the faster it accelerates — because your interest starts earning interest. The last 10 years of a 30-year plan often produce more growth than the first 20 combined.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Waiting to start

Someone who invests $200/month from age 25 to 35 (10 years) ends up with more at 65 than someone who invests $200/month from 35 to 65 (30 years). Time beats amount.

Chasing high returns

Chasing the hot stock or crypto of the month usually backfires. Consistent contributions to low-cost index funds over decades beat market timing.
