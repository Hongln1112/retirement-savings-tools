Why an emergency fund matters

Life happens: a medical bill, a car breakdown, a sudden job loss. Without a buffer, you're forced to put it on a credit card or dip into retirement savings — both expensive mistakes.

The standard advice is 3–6 months of expenses, but if you're in a volatile industry or have dependents, 9–12 months is safer. Keep it in a high-yield savings account, not invested — you need it liquid.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Investing the emergency fund

Your emergency fund must be liquid — you might need it during a market crash. Don't put it in stocks or crypto. A high-yield savings account is boring but correct.

Confusing wants with emergencies

A sale on a TV is not an emergency. A vacation is not an emergency. If you can plan for it, it belongs in your regular budget, not the emergency fund.

FAQ
